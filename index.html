Введение

Donald Ervin Knuth (English: Donald Ervin Knuth, IPA; born (February 1939) January 1938, Milwaukee, Wisconsin) is an American computer scientist, Doctor of Philosophy (1963), emeritus professor at Stanford University, member of the American Philosophical Society (2012)[6], educator and ideologist of programming, author of 19 monographs (including a number of classic books on programming) and more than 160 articles, and developer of several well-known software technologies. He is the author of the world-famous book series devoted to fundamental algorithms and methods of computational mathematics, as well as the creator of the desktop publishing systems TeX and METAFONT, designed for typesetting and layout of books on scientific and technical topics (primarily physics and mathematics).
